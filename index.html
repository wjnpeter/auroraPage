Privacy Policy – App Studio PC Paid iOS Applications

Effective Date: January 13, 2024

At App Studio PC, we are committed to delivering high-quality, professional iOS applications that respect and protect your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle personal data collected through our premium, ad-free apps. Our users' trust is our top priority, and we take privacy very seriously.

1. Data Collection

We collect the following types of personal data, either directly from you, via device permissions, or through third-party services:

  • Location Data: Both approximate and precise location (with your explicit consent), collected continuously to power location-based features.
  • Usage Data: Such as IP address, device type, operating system, error logs, and general app usage statistics.
  • Device Identifiers: A unique installation-based UUID is stored to help with analytics and user preferences.
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: Used to support core functionality and performance monitoring. We do not use tracking for advertising purposes.

We use your personal data exclusively to enhance your experience with our professional-grade apps:

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We only process personal data when we have a lawful basis to do so, such as:

  • Your explicit consent
  • Contractual necessity

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. UUIDs and location data are stored until you delete the app or revoke permissions. After this period, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

5. Data Sharing and Transfers

Your data is never sold. It may be shared with trusted third-party service providers (e.g., secure analytics platforms or cloud hosting services) strictly under confidentiality agreements. If data is transferred outside your jurisdiction, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

6. User Rights

To exercise your rights or ask questions about your data, contact us at appstudio.pc@gmail.com. We are committed to transparency and will respond promptly.

You can enable or disable these permissions via your device’s system settings at any time.
